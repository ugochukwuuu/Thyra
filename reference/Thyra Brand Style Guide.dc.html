02 Voice & Tone

Lead with the win. Make the outcome impossible to ignore.

Thyra sells. We open with the result the reader wants, put a number on it, and remove every reason to wait. Every headline promises an outcome, not a feature. We're bold, specific, and energetic — but we only make claims the store can back up. Confidence, not exaggeration.

Outcome-first

Open with the result — more sales, more orders, more customers. Features come after the payoff, never before it.

Specific

Real numbers beat vague adjectives. “3X your monthly orders” lands; “grow your business” doesn't.

High-energy

Punchy, active, momentum-driven. We push the reader toward the next step and remove every excuse to stall.

We say
  • "Sell 3X the products you've ever moved in a month."
  • "Get customers ordering without ever opening the store."
  • "A high-converting store + paid ads, without breaking the bank."
We avoid
  • "We build ecommerce websites." (feature, no payoff)
  • "Revolutionary next-gen synergy platform." (empty hype)
  • "Grow your business." (vague — grow by how much?)
03 Color

A warm, quiet palette with one deliberate spark.

Cream and charcoal do the heavy lifting and alternate down the page. Terracotta is the accent — used sparingly on actions and markers, never as a background fill.
